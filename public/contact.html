We’d love to hear from you

Questions about an order, your Membership Pack, Nooks or Nookaa Money? Want to share feedback or make a privacy request? Write to us and our team will get back to you.

operations@nookaa.in

For quick help

If you are already a Nookaa customer, the fastest way to reach us is Help & Support inside the Nookaa app. If you are at one of our outlets, our team there can help you right away, including with a wrong, missing or spilled drink.

What to include in your email

  • The mobile number registered to your Nookaa account.
  • Your order details, such as the outlet, table, date and time, if your question is about an order.
  • A short description of the issue, and a screenshot if it helps.

Please never send your OTP, card, UPI or bank details by email. Nookaa staff will never ask for them.

When you will hear from us

We will acknowledge your email within 48 hours and aim to resolve your concern within 30 days.

Privacy and account requests

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw a consent you have given, through the app’s settings or by emailing us. For privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy. To delete your account and data, see how to delete your account.

Complaints and grievances

If you are not happy with how we have handled something, email us with the subject line Grievance. If your complaint is about your personal data and we have not resolved it, you can also approach the Data Protection Board of India. You may also contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915). Our Terms & Conditions explain how disputes are handled.
